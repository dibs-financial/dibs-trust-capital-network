# DIBS Trust Capital Network — Architecture & Blueprint

This document is the comprehensive technical blueprint for the DIBS platform. It covers:

1. **Identity and Operating Thesis** — Platform identity, system layers, product thesis, and non-negotiable operating rules
2. **Enterprise Controlled-Capital System** — Market wedge, customer classes, pain points, and product sequence
3. **Capital Authorization Architecture** — Policy decision layer, authorization objects, and access controls
4. **Evidence-Gated Capital Release** — Evidence requirements, validation, preconditions, and release outcomes
5. **Covenant Monitoring** — 24+ covenant categories, state model, and engine requirements
6. **Collateral Monitoring** — Record requirements, risk controls, and release dependencies
7. **VRDCT Trust and Risk-Intelligence Layer** — Signal types, data loop, and trust-score constraints
8. **Personal Policy-Loan Arbitrage** — Policy lifecycle, subsystem components, arbitrage mechanics, and risk controls
9. **Structured-Credit and Tranche Protocol** — Sentinel/Catalyst vaults, capital waterfall, dynamic capital floor, and reserve-rebuild constraints
10. **ERC-4626 Vault Security** — Donation/inflation attack vectors and virtual-offset mitigation
11. **Yield Routing** — Morpho Blue and Pendle PT/YT integration parameters and risks
12. **Pricing and Monetization** — Three-tier pricing, time-locked liquidity, Catalyst incentives, and migration programs
13. **Regulatory, Tax, and Legal Boundaries** — RWA requirements, QOF structures, and bankruptcy-remoteness conditions
14. **Financial Model and Valuation** — Revenue equations, five-year P&L, unit economics, AUM economics, and seed valuation framework
15. **Technical Repository Architecture** — Monorepo boundaries, core contract layers, and 20-step build order
16. **Security, Governance, and Operational Controls** — Smart-contract controls, key management, and data audit
17. **Execution Roadmap** — Phase 0 through Phase 3 (0–24 months)
