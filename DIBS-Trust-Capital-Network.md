# **Structured-Credit and Tranche Protocol**

## **Protocol Thesis**

### **Core Function**

- Convert eligible on-chain yield, private-credit cash flow, and tokenized RWA cash flow into risk-segmented capital exposures.
- Apply rules-based loss allocation.
- Create senior-priority and first-loss positions.
- Use reserve thresholds and withdrawal controls.
- Separate risk underwriting from capital-preservation-oriented exposure.

### **Economic Positioning**

- Do not describe the protocol as creating guaranteed principal protection.
- Do not describe senior yield as fixed absent enforceable obligations.
- Do not describe a smart contract as creating legal bankruptcy remoteness.
- Do not describe system behavior as deterministic in all economic outcomes.
- Describe the system as rules-based, loss-waterfalled, reserve-governed, and subject to stated risks.

## **Tranche Naming Standard**

### **Brand-Facing Names**

- **Sentinel Vault**:
  - Senior-priority vault class.
  - Target income.
  - First-loss-buffered exposure.
  - Liquidity subject to published gates.

- **Catalyst Vault**:
  - Subordinated first-loss vault class.
  - Residual-yield exposure.
  - High loss volatility.
  - Potentially delayed liquidity.
  - Recapitalization and dilution risk.

### **Formal Contract Names**

- **Senior Tranche, Class A**.
- **Subordinated Tranche, Class B**.

### **Deprecated Name**

- **Vanguard**:
  - Do not use in protocol branding.
  - Do not use in tranche labels.
  - Do not use in financial-product marketing.
  - The mark is associated with The Vanguard Group's financial, mutual-fund, brokerage, investment, and asset-management services. [11][12]

## **Capital Waterfall**

### **Cash-Flow Order**

1. Receive portfolio cash flows, collateral proceeds, recoveries, and realized strategy yield.
2. Recognize expenses.
3. Recognize servicing costs.
4. Recognize realized losses.
5. Fund required reserves.
6. Accrue protocol fees according to published rules.
7. Distribute to Sentinel according to senior priority, available liquidity, and pool state.
8. Distribute residual value to Catalyst only if reserve conditions permit.
9. Apply realized losses to Catalyst NAV first.
10. Apply remaining losses to Sentinel only after Catalyst capital is exhausted.

### **Sentinel Attributes**

- Senior-priority economic claim.
- Target yield, not guaranteed yield.
- Subject to pool liquidity.
- Subject to redemption queue.
- Subject to reserve state.
- Subject to strategy and counterparty risk.
- Subject to smart-contract and oracle risk.
- Subject to legal and asset-servicing risk.

### **Catalyst Attributes**

- First-loss capital buffer.
- Residual-yield claim.
- Absorbs losses before Sentinel.
- Receives distribution only after costs, reserves, fees, and Sentinel obligations.
- May be subject to distribution suspension.
- May be diluted through recapitalization.
- May be exposed to full loss.

## **Dynamic Capital Floor**

### **Capital Ratio**

$$
JuniorRatio = \frac{NAV_{Catalyst}}{NAV_{Sentinel} + NAV_{Catalyst}}
$$

### **Minimum Ratio**

$$
JuniorRatio \geq MinJuniorRatio
$$

### **Initial Parameter Range**

- Expected initial `MinJuniorRatio`: 20%–30%.
- Do not treat 20%–30% as universally sufficient.
- Calibrate by:
  - Asset duration.
  - Liquidity.
  - Default probability.
  - Loss-given-default.
  - Collateral volatility.
  - Oracle quality.
  - Concentration.
  - Leverage.
  - Liquidation reliability.
  - Jurisdiction.
  - Legal enforceability.
  - Recovery timeline.

### **Capital Preservation Mode**

- Trigger when `JuniorRatio < MinJuniorRatio`.
- Queue, cap, or pause Sentinel withdrawals.
- Suspend Catalyst distributions.
- Restrict new Sentinel deposits if dilution would reduce coverage.
- Retain distributable yield for reserve rebuilding.
- Permit Catalyst recapitalization under disclosed pricing rules.
- Publish current ratio.
- Publish required ratio.
- Publish reserve shortfall.
- Publish liquidity state.
- Publish withdrawal queue.
- Publish strategy health.
- Publish oracle state.
- Emit immutable state-transition events.

### **Reserve-Rebuild Constraint**

- Do not simply route all yield into a freely redeemable Catalyst vault.
- A freely redeemable Catalyst balance may inflate reported junior NAV without creating durable Sentinel protection.
- Route reserve-rebuild value to:
  - Segregated reserve accounting.
  - Non-distributable reserve shares.
  - Locked recapitalization balance.
  - Contract-enforced retained earnings account.
- Release reserve only after ratio restoration and defined liquidity tests.

$$
ReserveRelease =
\begin{cases}
0, & JuniorRatio < MinJuniorRatio \\
Eligible, & JuniorRatio \geq MinJuniorRatio \text{ and liquidity tests pass}
\end{cases}
$$

# **ERC-4626 Vault Security Architecture**

## **Tokenized Vault Core**

### **Standard**

- Use ERC-4626-compatible vault interfaces.
- Use ERC-20 share accounting.
- Use standardized deposit, mint, withdraw, redeem, preview, and asset-conversion functions.
- Maintain compatibility with controlled composability.
- Do not assume ERC-4626 itself solves tranche accounting, legal segregation, liquidity, valuation, or risk management.

## **Donation and Inflation Attack**

### **Attack Sequence**

1. Attacker deposits minimal assets into an empty or low-liquidity vault.
2. Attacker receives a minimal number of shares.
3. Attacker donates assets directly to the vault.
4. Donation changes apparent share-to-asset exchange rate.
5. Later depositor receives too few shares because of rounding.
6. In extreme cases, later depositor may receive zero shares.
7. Attacker extracts value through manipulated share ownership.

### **Virtual Offset Mitigation**

- Use virtual assets.
- Use virtual shares.
- Use increased share precision.
- Configure `_decimalsOffset()`.
- Establish a defined initial exchange rate.
- Increase attacker cost relative to potential precision gain.
- Reduce donation attack profitability. [13][14][15]

$$
Shares = Assets \times
\frac{TotalSupply + 10^{offset}}
{TotalAssets + 1}
$$

### **Required Security Statement**

> Vaults use virtual assets, virtual shares, and a configurable decimals offset to materially reduce the economic viability of ERC-4626 donation and rounding attacks.

### **Prohibited Security Statements**

- "Zero gas overhead" without measurement.
- "Fully neutralized" without proof.
- "No inflation attack possible."
- "Audited means safe."
- "Immutable means risk-free."

### **Additional Vault Controls**

- Non-redeemable seed liquidity.
- Meaningful minimum initial deposit.
- `minSharesOut` enforcement.
- Deposit slippage controls.
- Internal asset accounting where direct transfers distort `totalAssets()`.
- Deposit caps.
- Per-transaction rate limits.
- Strategy allocation caps.
- Emergency pause.
- Timelocked parameter changes.
- Invariant testing.
- Fuzz testing.
- Donation attack testing.
- Oracle-failure testing.
- Default and recovery testing.
- Reserve-rebuild testing.
- Withdrawal-queue testing.
- Recapitalization testing.

RAYE = \frac{Net\ Yield}{Economic\ Capital\ at\ Risk}
$$

- **Economic Capital at Risk**: Primarily Catalyst capital plus modeled residual Sentinel exposure.
- **Protocol Objective**: Improve risk-adjusted yield without undercapitalizing the first-loss buffer.
